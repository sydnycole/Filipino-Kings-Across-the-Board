About Filipino Kings Across the Board

Celebrating Philippine Chess Heritage Strategy

Designed for players, learners, and fans of the board, Filipino Kings Across the Board brings together a playable visual directory of 19 Filipino Chess Grandmasters and interactive guides to rules, special moves, and tactics all in one place.

How to Explore the Website

Step-by-Step User Guide

  1. Open the Interactive Chessboard to view all 32 starting pieces.
  2. Click any of the 19 circular competitor portraits to inspect their FIDE rating, biography, and top 10 openings.
  3. Click any of the 13 classic chess pieces to learn special rules like En Passant, Castling, and tactical forks.
  4. Click the Queen (♕) button in the top-left corner to open the slide-out piece movement guide.
  5. Visit the Grandmasters Gallery to browse all 19 portraits and jump directly to any player's profile on the board.

Key Project Features Data Sources

Technologies Used
  • HTML5: Semantic structure across 3 linked pages (index.html, gallery.html, and about.html).
  • CSS3: Modular external stylesheets (style.css, modal.css, and pages.css) using CSS Grid and Flexbox.
  • JavaScript: Dynamic 8x8 board generation, interactive tabbed modals, and modular data files.
Official Websites References Used
  • FIDE Ratings Database (ratings.fide.com) — Official FIDE IDs, titles, federations, and peak/current ratings.
  • ChessBase Players Database (players.chessbase.com) — Player career profiles, active years, and historical game records.
  • Chessgames.com (chessgames.com) — Total games played, win ratios, White/Black records, and top 10 openings.
  • Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org) — Used strictly as a source for archival player portrait photographs.
  • Chess.com (chess.com) — Tactical definitions (forks, pins, skewers, checkmate traps) and gameplay references.
  • Lichess (lichess.org) — Interactive tactics references, piece movement guides, and board UI inspiration.
